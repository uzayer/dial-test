Print & Editorial
Editorial Print Design
The same design language — tokens, type scale, color — extends to print: lab reports, grant proposals, conference posters, and academic publications. The main adaptation is swapping rem for pt, tightening leading, and applying proper print margins. Lora's ink traps and Source Sans 3's legibility hold well at 10–11pt on coated stock.

Research Overview
Technology and Community in Bangladesh
DIAL's research programme centres on communities whose relationship with digital technology has been shaped by structural constraints — limited literacy, intermittent connectivity, economic precarity, and social hierarchies that determine who speaks and who is listened to. Our methods are participatory by default: we co-design with communities rather than for them.
In 2024 the lab published 12 full papers at ACM CHI, CSCW, and USENIX SOUPS, with three receiving awards. Field research was conducted in Dhaka, Cox's Bazar, and Sylhet, involving over 200 participants across domestic worker, garment worker, and student populations.
"We build from field realities, not lab assumptions."
— DIAL Research Principles

Screen → Print: key adaptations
Units
Replace rem with pt. Screen 1rem ≈ 14px → print body is 10.5 pt.
Leading
Screen line-height 1.7 → print leading 15pt (10.5 body). Tighter on print is correct.
Colour
Convert OKLCH/hex to CMYK. Navy 100/68/12/52. Proof on coated stock — navy can appear purple on uncoated.
Tracking
All-caps labels: +40 to +60 units. Running heads: +60. Body text: 0 (optical, not tracked).
Radius
Card radius (7px screen) → 2–3mm in print. Boxes and figure borders: 0.25pt stroke.
Hyphenation
Enable auto-hyphenation for body text in print layouts. Justified body is conventional in academic print but optional here.
